# Table Pulse Remote Reaction Minigames

Define the new remote reaction mini-games in concrete product language so they read like a flagship feature rather than a vague engagement promise.

## Goal

Give players a dramatic city-is-still-moving moment without breaking consent, authority, or campaign privacy.

## What They Are

Remote reaction mini-games are short governed follow-up encounters that occur after a
Table Pulse packet is emitted.

They are:

- opt-in or policy-allowed
- receipt-backed
- bounded in consequence
- safe to adjudicate outside the main table session

They are not:

- direct mutation of the table state
- autonomous side campaigns
- public scoreboards
- a replacement for the GM

## Core Mini-Game Families

### Intercept

The player receives a short intelligence or courier prompt and chooses how aggressively to intercept, forward, or suppress it.

Payoff:

- rumor gain
- heat shift
- faction notice

### Cover Story

The player gets a social or public-facing cleanup prompt and must pick a cover approach.

Payoff:

- public fallout softening
- reputation lane protection
- possible contradiction receipt for later review

### Scramble

The player gets a time-sensitive logistics or support prompt.

Payoff:

- small resource preservation
- standing-order reinforcement
- opposition-clock movement

### Temptation

The player gets a morally gray opportunity prompt.

Payoff:

- faction favor
- pressure increase
- future story hook

### Shadow Reply

A player or faction representative sends back a coded answer to a pulse packet.

Payoff:

- role-path advancement
- Passport identity flavor
- follow-on rumor or order hook

## GM And Policy Rules

Every mini-game must inherit:

- campaign privacy posture
- recipient allowlist
- quiet-hours and mute rules
- GM override authority
- bounded consequence rules

Mini-games may:

- draft consequences
- move pressure
- create receipts
- recommend fallout

Mini-games may not:

- create public truth on their own
- reveal private session content by default
- alter campaign canon without approval

## Surface Requirements

### On Mobile / PWA

The prompt should feel:

- immediate
- sharp
- readable in under a minute

The player should see:

- why this arrived
- what is at stake
- how strong the consequence is
- whether the GM must still approve fallout

### In Signal Deck

The mini-game should appear as:

- a live card
- visible pressure and countdown
- one strong dramatic choice and one safer choice

### In GM Cockpit

The GM should see:

- prompt source
- recipient reasoning
- chosen response
- proposed consequence
- proof chain

## Best-Case Pattern

The best version of this feature looks like:

1. a player gets a tight dramatic prompt
2. the choice feels sharp and immediate
3. the result lands in Passport or faction pressure
4. the city later reflects that move through rumor or newsroom fallout

That is the loop worth building toward.

Avoid:

- giant wall of text
- a generic push notification
- a random mini-quiz disconnected from world state

## Example Loop

After a high-heat extraction, a player receives a Scramble mini-game through Signal Deck. They choose to burn a favor to reroute a courier instead of going dark. That choice creates a receipt, nudges a faction pressure lane, preserves one standing-order asset, and later lets the newsroom surface show a subtle public-safe consequence instead of exposing the whole private session.

## Readiness Bar

Use stronger launch language only when:

1. the prompt is dramatic in under one screen
2. the choice is meaningful but bounded
3. the consequence is receipt-backed
4. the result can feed Passport, rumor, faction, or newsroom lanes
5. the system still keeps GM and consent authority intact
